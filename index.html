How to swap ZEC from a shielded wallet

Match the deposit’s address format

Zcash has transparent t-addresses and shielded z-addresses. Unified addresses can combine receiver types. Follow the order’s accepted formats; shielded wallet support alone does not establish deposit compatibility.

Know what remains public

Zcash uses zk-SNARKs to verify shielded transactions without exposing their private details. Transparent addresses reveal activity publicly. Swapping into BTC, USDT or ETH does not carry Zcash’s shielded privacy onto the receiving network.

ZEC swap taking too long? How to track it

Address rejected

Check the full address and accepted Zcash format. For USDT or ETH payouts, verify the destination network before creating the order again.

ZEC swap still pending

Find your transaction ID in the sending wallet and check its confirmations. If the deposit is confirmed but the order has stalled, contact the service with the order ID and transaction ID.

Quote expired or deposit too small

Refresh the quote before sending. Check the minimum after any wallet withdrawal fee. If you already sent, use the existing order’s support route before sending more.

Where can I swap ZEC to BTC, USDT or ETH?

Can I swap ZEC from a shielded address?

Check the service’s deposit instructions for shielded sender and address-format support. Use a compatible Zcash wallet and the exact deposit address assigned to your order.

How long does it take to swap ZEC?

The total time includes Zcash deposit confirmations, order processing and confirmation on the receiving network. Use your order status and transaction IDs to track each stage.

How do I get the best swap ZEC rate?

Compare the final receive amount for the same ZEC amount and destination network. Include the exchange fee, spread and payout fee, then check the quote’s expiry and rate conditions.

Why is my swap ZEC deposit not showing?

Check that the wallet broadcast the transaction, then verify the deposit address, amount and confirmations. If it is confirmed but missing from the order, contact the service with your order ID and transaction ID.
