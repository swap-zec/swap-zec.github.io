What is ZEC

What a Zcash swap is

A Zcash swap exchanges ZEC for another coin, or another coin for ZEC, through an instant-exchange route. Zcash is its own blockchain with its own coin, so the swap is not a token trade on a DEX: it is a cross-chain exchange between two networks.

What ZEC is

ZEC is the native coin of Zcash, a proof-of-work network based on Bitcoin's design that adds optional shielded transactions. Shielded transactions use zero-knowledge proofs, so the sender, receiver and amount do not appear on the public ledger.

How a route works

You pick the coin you send and the coin you want, enter an amount and a payout address. The route gives you a one-time deposit address. You send funds to it from your own wallet, and the route pays the result to the address you provided.

Transparent vs shielded

Transparent vs shielded addresses

Zcash has two kinds of pool. Transparent addresses start with t and work like Bitcoin addresses. Shielded addresses start with z (older Sapling) or u (unified, which can bundle several receiver types in one string). Always check which type a route accepts, for both the deposit you send from and the payout you receive to.

In practice

If a route only takes a transparent deposit or payout address, the interface will say so. You can usually shield funds afterwards in your own wallet. Whether to use one type or the other is your decision, within the laws that apply to you.

How to swap

How to swap ZEC

Quote and deposit

The route shows an estimated amount you will receive before you send anything. Nothing is signed in the interface; you sign the transfer in your own wallet when you send to the deposit address.

Step by step

  1. Choose the coin you send and the coin you receive, for example ZEC to BTC, and enter the amount.
  2. Paste your payout address for the receiving coin and check it matches the right network and address type. Add a refund address if the route asks for one.
  3. Review the quote, the route's limits and the deposit address types it accepts, then create the exchange.
  4. Send the exact coin and amount to the deposit address from your own wallet. Do not reuse that address later.
  5. Wait for the required confirmations. The route then pays out to your address, and you can follow the status with the exchange ID.

Keep your refund address current: if a deposit is late, too small or sent to a route that has expired, the refund goes there.

Fees

What a Zcash swap costs

Network fee on ZEC

Sending ZEC uses a small network fee, paid in ZEC and deducted by your wallet when you send. It is usually small compared with the swap amount, and the exact value depends on your wallet and the transaction.

Route cost

The quoted amount already includes the route's exchange spread or service fee and the network fee for the payout on the other chain. Read the estimated receive amount, not just the rate. Fixed-rate and floating-rate routes differ in whether the final amount can change.

Minimums and limits

Each route sets a minimum and maximum amount per exchange. Sending less than the minimum can delay the exchange or lead to a refund. Learn more about the network in the Zcash documentation.

Security

Staying safe

Non-custodial by design

This interface does not connect to your wallet and does not hold private keys or funds. You keep control of your own wallets and send from them yourself; the interface only shows quotes and a deposit address.

Before you send

  • Check the deposit address and its type carefully, and send only the coin and network shown for that exchange.
  • Confirm your payout and refund addresses belong to wallets you control and match the correct network.
  • Test with a small amount first if you are new to a route.
  • Wait for confirmations before assuming the deposit has been received.
  • Follow the laws and tax rules where you live. Exchange routes may apply their own checks and can hold or refuse transactions.
FAQ

Zcash Swap FAQ

How does a Zcash swap work?

You choose the coins, enter a payout address and receive a one-time deposit address for the route. You send funds to it from your own wallet, and the route pays the exchanged coin to your payout address after the deposit is confirmed.

Can I use a shielded address for a Zcash swap?

It depends on the route. Some accept shielded (z- or unified u-) addresses for deposit or payout, and others only accept transparent t- addresses. Check the accepted address types shown for your route before you send.

What is the refund address for?

If an exchange cannot be completed, for example when a deposit is below the minimum or arrives after the route expires, the funds are returned to the refund address. Use an address you control on the same network as the coin you send.

How many confirmations does a ZEC deposit need?

Each route sets its own number of network confirmations before it starts the payout. The exchange status shows when the deposit is detected and when it is confirmed, so wait for that rather than assuming a fixed time.

What does it cost to send ZEC?

Sending ZEC carries a small network fee paid in ZEC and taken by your wallet. The swap quote separately includes the route's own fee, so compare the estimated amount you receive.

Does this Zcash swap interface hold my coins or keys?

No. It is a non-custodial interface: it never holds private keys or your funds. You send from your own wallet to the deposit address and receive at your own payout address.
